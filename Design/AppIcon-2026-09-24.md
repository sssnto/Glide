# Glide 应用图标

最终图标：`Glide/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`（1024 × 1024、不透明 RGB）。原始生成稿：`Design/AppIcon-Glide-2026-09-24-original.png`。旧图标备份：`Design/AppIcon-legacy-2026-09-24.png`。

概念：将“Glide”的首字母抽象成一笔连续的滑动轨迹。深色背景承接应用影院界面，雾绿符号对应播放主动作色；不用通用播放三角形，也不依赖小尺寸难辨认的细节。最终图标使用内置 imagegen 工具生成，再按 iOS 资产目录要求缩至 1024 × 1024。

最终生成提示词：

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final square iOS app icon artwork, 1024×1024 pixels, for a premium personal video library app named Glide. Create a distinctive pure flat 2D geometric monogram, not a play button: one continuous pale sage-green thick stroke forms an abstract lowercase g made from a nearly closed rounded-square loop with a small open gap at the right, then a short smooth curved tail sweeping down and to the right. The open gap and tail imply sliding motion. One shape only, absolutely no layered cards or stacked frames. Strong calm silhouette recognizable at tiny app-icon size. Background is uniform opaque near-black forest green #0C100F, entire full-bleed square. The monogram color is uniform #C5DEC5. Center the monogram optically at about 56% of canvas width, generous empty space around it. Stroke ends are clean and slightly rounded. Mathematically crisp edges, premium Swiss graphic design, balanced negative space. No triangles, arrows, player controls, filmstrips, cameras, buttons, multiple frames, extra objects, letters typed as font, words, gradients, glows, shadows, highlights, texture, 3D, mockup, border or rounded outer corners. Exact flat graphic, no watermark.

校验：60 × 60 缩略预览清晰；iOS Release 无签名构建通过。尚未上传 TestFlight。
